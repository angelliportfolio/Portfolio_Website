The Photographer

My approach is built around honoring
the atmosphere of your day.

The light through a venue window at golden hour. The quiet before the first look. The way a moment breathes right before something important happens. These are the details I live for — the ones that, when captured, transform a photograph into a memory you can feel.

I photograph for couples who want imagery that is quietly luxurious. Editorial without being cold. Deeply personal, and always, unmistakably theirs.

Based in Colorado and available for destination weddings worldwide, I work with both film and digital, choosing the medium that best serves the light and the mood of each unique day.

— Angelli
